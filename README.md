# MenuMax

**Digital QR Menu, Table Ordering & Waiter Calling Platform for Restaurants**

MenuMax is a web-based digital menu platform designed for restaurants, cafés, bars and hospitality businesses.

Customers can access the restaurant menu instantly by scanning a QR code — no application installation is required.

## Features

- Digital QR menus
- Multilingual menu support
- Product photos, descriptions and prices
- Allergen information
- Table-based QR codes
- Table ordering
- Waiter calling
- Table management
- Instant menu and price updates
- Mobile-friendly interface
- No app installation required
- 0% commission on restaurant orders

## How It Works

1. The restaurant creates its digital menu.
2. Products, categories, prices and images are added.
3. MenuMax generates QR codes for restaurant tables.
4. Customers scan the QR code with their phones.
5. The digital menu opens directly in the browser.
6. Depending on the restaurant's configuration, customers can browse the menu, place an order or call a waiter.
